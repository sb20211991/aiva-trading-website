AIVA TRADING — TAP TO PAY vs WEBSITE CHECKOUT

These are two different Square products.

1) Website checkout (aivatrading.uk)
   - Uses Square Payment Links / hosted checkout via Cloudflare Pages Functions
   - Accepts Visa/Mastercard and, where eligible, Apple Pay and Google Pay
   - Customer pays in the browser — no Square hardware and no Tap to Pay
   - This is what the online shop basket uses
   - Stuck on a hardware shop page? See HARDWARE-BYPASS-GUIDE.txt — Skip / Not now

2) Tap to Pay on iPhone (in person)
   - Runs inside the Square Point of Sale app on an eligible iPhone
   - Does NOT need a Square card reader
   - If Square asks you to pick a physical reader, choose Tap to Pay / skip reader
     (wording varies by app version), or open Square POS → More → Settings →
     Account → Tap to Pay on iPhone and turn it ON
   - Needs iPhone XS or newer, latest iOS, passcode, internet, Square UK account
   - This does not replace website checkout and is not controlled by aivatrading.uk

If you only need customers to pay on the website, ignore the card-reader setup and
finish Square sandbox + Cloudflare Functions deploy for Payment Links instead.
Do not purchase Square Reader or Terminal for aivatrading.uk.
