COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you enquire

Can I request a product I don’t see listed?

Choose Other sourcing enquiry and describe the product or include a model number.

Where are the prices and minimum order quantities?

Availability, pricing and minimum quantities are confirmed for each enquiry and depend on the product and supplier.

What should I include in my sourcing brief?

Start with the product, quantity and delivery country. Add any important specifications, budget or target date. For samples, choose Request a sample in the navigation to prefill your enquiry.

INFORMED SOURCING

Know what you are buying.
Before you commit.

Use your enquiry to establish the product, supplier and delivery details. Availability, documentation and commercial terms must be confirmed for the specific quotation.

01 / The exact product

Ask for the model, current product photographs, specifications and sample options. Category images on this website illustrate sourcing interests; they do not confirm stock or an exact model.

02 / The supporting evidence

Request relevant test reports, certificates and inspection options for your product and destination. Check that documents match the model and manufacturer being quoted.

03 / The commercial terms

Confirm quantity, price, delivery terms, estimated timing and warranty arrangements in writing before ordering.
