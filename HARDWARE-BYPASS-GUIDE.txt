AIVA TRADING LIMITED — SKIP SQUARE HARDWARE (ONLINE ONLY)
Updated: 15 November 2026 (Europe/London)

VERDICT
You do NOT need Square Reader, Square Terminal, Tap to Pay hardware, or any
physical card machine for customers to pay on aivatrading.uk.

Website checkout uses Square-hosted Payment Links (API). Card / Apple Pay /
Google Pay (when eligible) happen in the browser. Hardware selection is a
signup upsell, not a technical requirement for online payments.

==================================================
HOW TO GET PAST THE HARDWARE SELECTION PAGE
==================================================

1) Prefer desktop Square Dashboard over the mobile POS app
   https://squareup.com/dashboard
   The POS app is built around in-person selling and pushes readers harder.

2) On “Choose your hardware / Get a reader / Order Terminal”
   Look for: Skip · Not now · No thanks · Continue without hardware · X / Close
   Do not buy anything. Leaving without a reader is normal for online sellers.

3) Go straight to online payments proof
   Dashboard → Payments (or Payments & orders) → Payment links → Create link
   Creating a Payment Link (even £1, inactive later) confirms online checkout
   works without hardware.
   Official UK guide:
   https://squareup.com/help/gb/en/article/6692-get-started-with-square-checkout-links

4) Get developer credentials (needed for aivatrading.uk)
   https://developer.squareup.com/apps
   Create/open an application → Credentials → Sandbox Access Token
   Locations → Sandbox Location ID
   Put those values in Cursor secrets (never in chat).

5) If you are truly stuck with no Skip control
   - Open Dashboard in a fresh browser tab/window
   - Or use Square Support chat and say:
     “Aiva Trading Limited only needs online Payment Links for aivatrading.uk.
      We do not want a Reader or Terminal.”
   - Do not purchase hardware to “unblock” the account — it is not required

==================================================
WHAT HARDWARE IS FOR (OPTIONAL, SEPARATE)
==================================================

- Square Reader / Terminal = in-person shop / market stall
- Tap to Pay on iPhone = in-person POS, no reader, still not website checkout
- Website basket on aivatrading.uk = Payment Links / hosted Checkout only

See also: TAP-TO-PAY-AND-IN-PERSON.txt

==================================================
ELIGIBILITY FOR ONLINE PAYMENTS (UK)
==================================================

UK Square accounts can take online card payments via Payment Links without
buying hardware. What Square still requires from you (Dashboard only):

- Business / identity verification prompts completed
- Active selling Location
- UK bank account for payouts
- (For our site) Developer app + sandbox token + webhook signature key

This agent cannot log into your Square Dashboard. After you add sandbox
secrets, run: npm run website:square-check
That checks location status + CREDIT_CARD_PROCESSING capability (no charges).
