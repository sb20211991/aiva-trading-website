A CLEARER BUYING PROCESS

Ask the right questions.
Order with clarity.

A practical checklist for your sourcing conversation. Each product and destination needs its own evidence and agreed terms.

01

Identify the exact product

Record manufacturer, model, condition and intended use. Ask for current product photographs and the specification sheet. A category image alone does not identify the goods you will receive.

02

Check a sample

Discuss sample availability, price, shipping and the checks you want to perform. Confirm whether the sample matches the proposed production model and what could change for a bulk order.

Request a sample quotation →
03

Match documents to the model

Ask which requirements apply to the product and destination. Review the manufacturer name, model, document issuer, scope and date. Generic logos or a certificate for a different model are not enough.

04

Agree inspection arrangements

Confirm whether inspection is included or separately arranged, who performs it, what is checked and when. Define what happens if the agreed checks identify a problem.

05

Confirm the complete price

Record quantity, currency, product cost, delivery terms, shipping charges and any taxes or duties included or excluded. Confirm the payment recipient and quotation reference before paying.

06

Keep the written agreement

Keep the specification, quotation, agreed dispatch estimate, warranty and returns terms, payment record and correspondence. Ask Aiva about anything that is unclear before ordering.
