Website privacy information

Enquiries and supplier offers

The enquiry and quote tools prepare drafts in your browser. You can copy or download the text or open it in email or WhatsApp. When you send a message, Aiva Trading Limited uses the information you provide to respond. Your chosen messaging service handles the message according to its own privacy terms.

Browsing the website

The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site. Product search and filters run in your browser.

Contact information in your draft

The forms ask for your name and reply email so Aiva can respond to your message. Company, product and delivery details help explain your request. Draft text is not automatically submitted by this website or saved to browser storage. It is shared when you send it using your chosen email or messaging service. Downloaded copies remain on your device until you delete them.

Questions about your information
