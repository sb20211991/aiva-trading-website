Language & translation

Choose your languages, then type or tap Start and speak for up to 2 minutes. Pauses are fine. Translations appear right here in Aiva and can be read aloud in a natural voice. The built-in phrasebook works offline.

Translation
Translations are processed by Aiva's secure server using a third-party translation engine and need an internet connection. Voice input uses your browser's speech recognition; if it is unavailable, you can always type. Read aloud uses a natural AI voice where one is available for the language, otherwise the best voice on your device.

Two-way conversation

Two people, two languages. Each person taps their own microphone and speaks. Aiva translates and reads it aloud for the other person.
