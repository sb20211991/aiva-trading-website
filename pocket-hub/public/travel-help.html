Travel booking help

Aiva Trading Limited operates Aiva Pocket Hub. For booking help, email Aivatradingltd2026@gmail.com or call +44 7833 013040. Include your booking reference, travel date and route. Keep card numbers, security codes and passport scans out of email.

Flights

For a flight booked inside Aiva, open My bookings and check the latest airline status. If payment appears on your card and confirmation is missing, contact Aiva before paying again. If you checked out on an airline website, use the booking reference and support details in the airline’s confirmation.

Train journeys

A timetable gives you a route and departure time. An in-app ticket requires a fare and a ticket issued by the distributor. An order reference alone cannot be used for travel. For urgent help at a station, speak to the operator before boarding.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

For an in-app booking, contact Aiva with your reference and the change you need. Eligibility and any fee depend on the carrier and fare conditions. If departure is close, also contact the carrier using the details in your confirmation.

Passenger information

For an in-app Nuitee flight booking, Aiva sends traveller and contact details to Nuitee to process the booking. The provider’s payment form handles card entry. The app saves booking information in this browser so you can find the reference again. Keep a separate copy of your confirmation before clearing browser data.
